Workout and macro tracking that works offline. No ads, no subscription.

Download for Android

Version 1.3.0 · 18 MB · needs Android 8.0 or newer

Installing it

  1. Tap Download above.
  2. When it finishes, tap the downloaded file (check your notifications or Downloads).
  3. Android will warn that this did not come from the Play Store and ask whether to allow installs from your browser. Tap Settings, turn the switch on, then come back and tap Install.
  4. The icon appears with your other apps.

That warning is normal for any app installed outside the Play Store — it is Android telling you it has not checked this app, not telling you something is wrong. If you would rather not, wait for the Play Store version.

What it does

  • Tracks your lifts and works out when you are ready to add weight or move up a level
  • Water, protein, carbs and fat — calories are calculated for you
  • A calendar that fills in as you hit the day’s goals
  • A home screen widget for logging without opening the app
  • Everything is stored on your phone and works with no account at all
  • Optional: sign in with Google to carry your history to a new phone

It starts empty

That is on purpose — it is your routine, not someone else’s. Add exercises yourself, or open the Coach tab, send your data to any AI assistant, and paste the plan it writes straight back in. There is also an example routine in Settings if you would rather edit something than start from scratch.
