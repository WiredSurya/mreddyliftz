## Before uploading to Play

- Play needs **at least 2** phone screenshots, min 1080px on the short side. These qualify.
- `04_progress.png` should be recaptured after a few sessions are logged — an empty page is
  honest in the app but sells nothing on a store listing.
- The status bar shows a real clock and battery. Fine for Play, but crop or use a clean
  status bar if you want them to look styled.
- No feature graphic (1024x500) yet. That is still required and is not a screenshot.
